# EcoRevive

Point your camera at a piece of waste. EcoRevive detects plastic, glass, metal, organic and hazardous items and shows a short upcycling idea or safe disposal steps.

**Model status: preview.** The detector has not yet passed its final held-out evaluation, so treat its results as suggestions. A photo cannot confirm whether an item is safe to handle.
